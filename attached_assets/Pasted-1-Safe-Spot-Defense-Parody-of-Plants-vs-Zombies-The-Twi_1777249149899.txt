1. "Safe Spot Defense" (Parody of Plants vs. Zombies)
• The Twist: Instead of zombies, your apartment is being invaded by "Toxic Personalities" (The Energy Vampire, The Over-Sharer, The Gaslighter).
• Mechanic: You place "Boundaries" (like a 'Mute Button' or 'Physical Distance') to stop them from reaching your couch and draining your "Sanity Meter."
• Noir Vibe: Set in a rain-slicked apartment with blue/purple neon lighting.
2. "Shade Thrower" (Parody of Angry Birds)
• The Twist: You launch "Passive-Aggressive Comments" at "Clout Chasers" sitting on fragile glass pedestals.
• Mechanic: Aim and fire different types of "Shade" (The Backhanded Compliment, The 'K', The Unfollow) to topple their social standing.
• Noir Vibe: The "birds" are actually crumpled-up notes or dark silhouettes of the protagonist.
3. "Vibe Check" (Parody of Fruit Ninja)
• The Twist: Instead of fruit, you are slicing through "Red Flags" and "Fake Profiles."
• Mechanic: Slicing a "Real Connection" is like hitting a bomb—it ends the game because it's "too scary." Slicing "Red Flags" gives you "Safety Points."
• Noir Vibe: High-speed slashing effects in neon pink against a pitch-black background.
4. "Social Ladder" (Parody of Doodle Jump)
• The Twist: You are literally jumping up a ladder of "Trending Topics."
• Mechanic: Some platforms are "Canceled"—if you step on them, they break instantly. You have to jump on "Viral" platforms to get a boost.
• Noir Vibe: The higher you go, the more distorted and "glitchy" the world becomes.
5. "Follower Farm" (Parody of Farmville)
• The Twist: You aren't growing crops; you're growing "Clickbait."
• Mechanic: Plant a "Controversial Opinion" or a "Thirst Trap." If you don't "Water" them with "Engagement" every few hours, your followers wither away and leave you.
• Noir Vibe: Gritty, pixelated dirt and rows of glowing smartphone screens instead of plants.
6. "Word-Low" (Parody of Wordle)
• The Twist: Guess the "Buzzword" of the day to fit in at the underground bar.
• Mechanic: If you fail to guess the 5-letter social buzzword, you are "Exposed" as a poser and lose your "Coolness" points.
• Noir Vibe: A typewriter-style interface in a smoky room.
7. "Ghost Hunter" (Parody of Pac-Man)
• The Twist: You are navigating a maze of "Unanswered DMs."
• Mechanic: You are being chased by "Ghosts" (literally ghosts of people you've ghosted). Collect "Blue Checkmarks" to turn the tables and "Ignore" them.
• Noir Vibe: Neon blue maze lines in the dark parking garage.
